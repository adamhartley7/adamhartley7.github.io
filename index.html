02 - TopOS / Road Infrastructure

Concept

TopOS lays the roads.

TopOS is the infrastructure. It is a proposed local-first intranet road system for the AI agents operating inside a business.

01

Roads

Connect authorised AI-agent cars to the work they are allowed to reach.

02

Depots

Keep business context and tools close to the journeys that need them.

03

Junctions

Apply rules when work moves between people, agents and systems.

04

Control room

Show which cars are moving, what they are carrying and where they are going.

The Google Calendar booking route will open here once the schedule and availability are ready. TopOS is not yet built, shipped or validated.

03 - Already Using AI / Products

Measure the journey, then improve the traffic.

Integration comes first. You need to see the road network before you tune the traffic moving through it.

01

Icarus

Taximeter + fuel gauge

Icarus is the proposed estimating layer: understand the cost of a journey and, eventually, estimate the next one before the car leaves. A runnable internal v1 is in validation. It is not yet a verified public pre-run product.

In validation
02

Daedalus

More efficient engine

Daedalus is the proposed optimisation layer for model substitution and routing. The aim is to find a more efficient engine for the job without losing sight of what the journey requires. It has not shipped or been benchmarked.

Planned R&D
03

Athena

Interactive satnav

Athena is the proposed personalisation and prompting layer: an interactive satnav that uses business intent and context to help plan the route. It has not shipped or been benchmarked.

Planned R&D

04 - AI & Engineering

See the rules of the road.

The live historical analyser processes selected Claude and ChatGPT exports locally in the browser. It does not require a developer account, a code change or an upload to TOP.

The wider system is explained above. Icarus is in validation. TopOS, Daedalus and Athena are concepts or planned research and development, not production infrastructure.

  • LocalSelected history is analysed on the device for the live report.
